GALACTIC ARCADE

The Arcade is available only after BBS authentication. Each game uses your
Mars userid automatically. There are no separate game passwords or accounts.

Oregon Trail
  Begin by buying oxen, food, ammunition, clothing, and spare parts. Use HELP
  at either the outfitting or trail prompt. Progress is saved after every
  accepted command. QUIT returns to the Arcade without abandoning the trip.

WOPR / Joshua
  Type HELP or LIST GAMES at the JOSHUA prompt. Only installed routines are
  listed. QUIT returns to the Arcade. The simulation has no external AI or
  network access and contains no real military targeting information.

Falken's Maze (inside WOPR / Joshua)
  From the JOSHUA prompt, type PLAY FALKEN'S MAZE. The Maze is not duplicated
  on the Galactic Arcade's main menu.
  Move with N, S, E, and W. LOOK examines a room. TAKE useful items, and use
  ANSWER when a terminal presents a puzzle. QUIT returns to JOSHUA.

Mars Hotel Chess
  Play complete tournament chess against Stockfish 19. Use the arrow keys and
  Enter to select a piece and highlighted destination, or press M and enter a
  move such as e4, Nf3, O-O, or e2e4. L cycles Cadet, Voyager, and Commander
  difficulty. N starts a new game after confirmation; R resigns after
  confirmation. The board, move history, and result persist with your account.
  The screen automatically changes between full 80-column and narrow-phone
  layouts without changing the rules or saved position.

Zork I
  This is the authentic Infocom release 119 story running under native Frotz.
  Commands include LOOK, INVENTORY, SAVE, RESTORE, RESTART, and QUIT. Saves
  belong to your Mars account and cannot be selected from another account.

Legend of the Red Dragon
  Authentic, registered LORD 4.02a runs as an isolated DOS door. Mars permits
  up to four concurrent LORD nodes. Your character and the shared world persist
  between visits; rankings and daily-turn rules remain inside LORD itself.

Scores and saves
  Mars-native games create Hall of Fame records and resume automatically from
  their last committed command. Zork uses its original SAVE and RESTORE
  commands. LORD preserves its native shared-world data, but its rankings remain
  inside LORD rather than the Mars Hall of Fame.

Safe exits
  Use each game's own QUIT command, or X from the Arcade. Closing SSH terminates
  the attached game process and releases its node without deleting saved state.

A half-forgotten school computer may still answer if you call it JOSHUA from
the Lobby.
