MESSAGE BOARDS

Boards are public community discussions. Select a board, move through its post
list with the Up/Down arrows, and press Enter to read. N from the Lobby scans unread
posts. C shows board totals and unread counts.

S opens the board chooser. Use Up/Down or Page Up/Page Down to browse every
board, or type part of its number, name, or description to filter immediately.
Enter chooses the highlighted board; Esc cancels and keeps the current board.

Within a board, P creates a post and R replies to the selected post. Replies use
the full-screen editor and include quoted context where appropriate. Permission
checks are performed by the board service, not merely hidden in the menu.

THE POST OFFICE

Mail is private between BBS accounts. The folder view shows unread state and
supports Inbox, Sent, and Drafts. C composes a new message. Recipient selection
can be filtered by typing, or browsed with an empty search. Reply goes directly
to the original sender; Forward asks for a new recipient. Replies and forwards
include readable message history.

FULL-SCREEN EDITOR

Arrow keys      Move through the whole message
Home / End      Move within the displayed line
Page Up/Down    Scroll a long message
Enter           Insert a new line
Backspace       Delete before the cursor
Delete          Delete at the cursor
F2              Begin Send/Post confirmation
F3              Save a mail draft
Esc or Ctrl-C   Cancel without sending

A period on a line is ordinary text; the historical dot-to-finish convention
is not used. Unknown terminal control sequences are rejected rather than saved
inside a message. Message-size limits are enforced by the authoritative service.

PRIVACY

An ordinary account may read only its own mailbox through normal operations.
Email addresses in profiles are private. Do not put secrets into board posts,
mail, drafts, or quoted history merely because the transport is encrypted.
NEW scans unread messages across every board in your scan set. On a board,
Visit marks its current messages read; Zap removes it from New scans, and the
same key restores it. A required board cannot be zapped. Read state belongs to
each individual message, so opening a newer post never hides an older unread
one.

Post owners can edit, move, or delete their own unprotected posts. Delegated
Board Managers can protect and moderate their board. The service checks every
operation even if a menu item is visible.

Post Office [G] sends group mail to as many as 16 selected local members.
Account signatures are appended only when a message or post is sent; drafts
remain clean and editable.
