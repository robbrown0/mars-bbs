MARS INFORMATION NETWORK

The Information Network is a collection of current reports delivered through
the BBS. Press D in the Lobby, then use keys 0 through 9. X returns to the
previous screen. Lists use the Up/Down arrow keys and Enter opens a selection.

The BBS never contacts a provider while drawing a terminal screen.  A separate
background collector retrieves bounded HTTPS responses, validates and cleans
them, and stores a local last-known-good cache.  Every report identifies its
source and update time.  STALE means the provider is temporarily unavailable
and the most recent successful report is being shown.  UNAVAILABLE means there
is no validated cached report.

WEATHER

Choose a location by city, state/province, country, or postal code.  The choice
is private to your BBS account.  Forecast data comes from Open-Meteo; active
United States warnings come from NOAA/National Weather Service.  Forecasts are
guidance, not emergency instructions.  Follow local authorities during severe
weather.

NEWS WIRES

Headlines and provider-supplied summaries are displayed with attribution.
SUMMARY means the BBS does not reproduce the publisher's full article.  No
paywall or article page is scraped.

SOLAR AND AMATEUR RADIO

The display uses observed NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center products.  It
does not invent band openings from a few indices.  The notes page explains the
limits of solar flux and Kp as propagation indicators.

EARTHQUAKES AND ISS

Earthquakes use official USGS feeds.  ISS positions and passes are predictions
calculated locally from current CelesTrak orbital elements, not live spacecraft
telemetry.  Set a Weather Center location before requesting local passes.

FINANCIAL MARKETS

Massive Basic supplies end-of-day values. The board shows the real Nasdaq
Composite plus clearly labelled DIA, SPY, and QQQ ETF proxies; those ETF prices
are not presented as the underlying Dow, S&P, or Nasdaq index values. Press L
to look up a U.S. stock or ETF symbol. Every screen identifies the delay and
source. Quotes are informational and are not investment advice.

MOVIES AND TELEVISION

The Screening Room lists recent and upcoming American film releases, new
television-series premieres, and returning seasons. Film dates come from the
current Wikipedia film calendar. Television schedules come from TVmaze and
include U.S. broadcast/cable plus globally scheduled streaming premieres.
Both sources are credited under their CC BY-SA terms. Announced dates can
change; this is a release calendar, not a guarantee of local availability.
