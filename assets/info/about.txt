WHAT THIS REVIVAL IS

Mars Hotel Revival is a private, terminal-first community inspired by The
Mars Hotel BBS at Mississippi State University. It preserves the character of
a dial-up-era BBS: a keyboard-driven Lobby, public boards, private mail, live
conversation, and a library meant for wandering.

The interface is intentionally text-mode. It is not a web forum wearing a
retro theme. Menus respond to single-letter commands, long material opens in
paged readers, and real-time conversation stays inside the terminal session.

WHAT YOU CAN DO HERE

* Read and reply on public message boards.
* Use the Post Office for private mail, sent mail, and drafts.
* Enter native Chat rooms with your authenticated BBS identity.
* Page an online member for one-to-one split-screen Direct Talk.
* Enter a separate, restricted local IRC service.
* Browse curated period texts and the surviving Mars FTP catalog.
* Maintain a display name, private email address, public profile, password,
  and personal time zone.

WHAT WAS PRESERVED

The project began by restoring Eagles BBS, a maintained descendant of Pirate
BBS, and by studying the recovered Pirate BBS 1.9 source. Pirate's own ReadMe
says that version was running on Mars Hotel on November 25, 1991. The Revival
keeps the historically important separation between Talk, native Chat, and
IRC, while preserving terminal navigation and upstream credits.

WHAT WAS RECONSTRUCTED

No production backup of Mars Hotel's boards, private mail, accounts, menus,
welcome artwork, or IRC configuration has been recovered. The hotel artwork,
Lobby wording, room names, polished mail client, and modern containment model
are Revival work. They are never presented as recovered Mars artifacts.

HOW THE MODERN SYSTEM DIFFERS

SSH supplies encrypted transport and a real PTY. BBS authentication still
happens at the familiar userid-or-new prompt. Authoritative accounts, boards,
mail, files, presence, native Chat, and IRC are separated into constrained
services instead of exposing one shared legacy filesystem to every session.

This improves containment but does not make the software vulnerability-free
or by itself settle Internet-ingress policy. The project remains honest about
tested boundaries, historical uncertainty, and unfinished work.

THE NAME

This system is Mars Hotel-inspired. It is not the recovered original site and
does not claim ownership of the original community's messages, identities, or
artwork. Its purpose is to remember how that kind of place felt while making a
small, useful terminal community for today.
